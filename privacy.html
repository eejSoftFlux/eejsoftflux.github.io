Word Match: Tile Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026
Platform: Android / Google Play
Package: com.eejsoftflux.wordmatch

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Word Match: Tile Puzzle (the “App”), developed by eejsoftflux (“we” or “us”). The App is intended for ages 6 and older, including children, teens, and adults. It covers local game information and the third-party services used for advertising, consent, purchases, and Google Play reviews.

Your game progress is stored on your device, and the App has no developer-operated account login or cloud-save service. However, Google and Unity services can collect and process information when they operate. Local game storage does not mean that the App collects or transmits no data.

1. Information handled by the App

The App processes information needed to run the game, save your choices, deliver rewards, and recognize purchases. This includes:

  • Current and completed levels, best score, daily play and completion history, and tutorial or review-prompt status.
  • Your selected age band (6–12, 13–17, or 18 and older), stored locally to apply age-related protection settings.
  • Coin balance, Undo and Shuffle item counts, sound and grayscale preferences, and the ad-removal entitlement.
  • Purchase receipts and transaction information supplied through Google Play and Unity IAP. A hash of each fulfilled purchase token is kept locally to help prevent duplicate rewards.

The App does not ask you to create an account with eejsoftflux or enter a name, email address, password, or date of birth to play. Its game code does not send your saved levels or inventory to a developer-operated server. The third-party processing described below is separate from this local game storage.

2. Google AdMob advertising

The App uses Google Mobile Ads for banners at the bottom of gameplay, interstitial ads after Next is selected following levels 6, 9, 12 and subsequent three-level intervals, and optional rewarded ads. Watch Ad grants 50 coins only when the SDK reports completion of the reward requirements. Closing an ad alone does not earn a reward.

Remove Ads removes banner and interstitial advertising; optional rewarded ads remain available. The integration supports live advertising and is not limited to test ads. Ads may be preloaded after age and consent checks, before you select Watch Ad. Remove Ads and choosing not to watch a rewarded ad therefore do not stop all advertising SDK processing.

Before the App initializes Mobile Ads or requests ads, it applies the following AgeRestrictedTreatment setting based on your selection:

  • Ages 6–12: Child, requesting Google's child protections, including restrictions on personalized advertising.
  • Ages 13–17: Teen, requesting Google's teen protections.
  • Ages 18 and older: Unspecified, with applicable privacy choices handled through UMP. This value does not itself mean consent has been granted.

All age bands use MaxAdContentRating.G to limit requested ad content to Google's general-audience rating. Age settings and this rating are ad-serving controls, not a guarantee that SDKs process no data. See Google's age-treatment and ad-content settings.

Google's Mobile Ads disclosure identifies data such as IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available. Google processes these data for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Processing depends on device settings, consent, and the ad service's configuration.

The game code does not itself read the advertising ID or request precise location. The Android build process removes the AD_ID permission and the AdServices advertising ID, attribution, and topics permissions. These restrictions do not mean that all identifiers, IP addresses, or SDK communications are blocked; behavior also depends on the Android version and installed build. Advertising SDKs can process identifiers and device information independently of that game code. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Privacy consent and Privacy options

The App waits for an age-band selection before starting its UMP flow, Mobile Ads initialization, ad requests, and Unity IAP initialization. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) updates consent information after that selection. For both ages 6–12 and 13–17, the App sets UMP's TagForUnderAgeOfConsent to true, conservatively suppressing consent solicitation for all under-18 users rather than assuming they meet their country's consent age. For adults it is false, and UMP presents applicable regional consent messages when required. This UMP flag is separate from Mobile Ads' Child or Teen setting. Advertising requests are allowed only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. Google may use stored consent choices and information needed to determine and apply the appropriate privacy message.

When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, a Privacy options button appears in the App's Settings panel. It opens Google's form so that you can review or change available choices. This button may not appear in every region or consent state.

These controls apply to Google's advertising consent flow. They are not a universal opt-out from Google Play purchase processing or Unity IAP service data. See Google's UMP guidance. Age selection and UMP are not identity verification or verifiable parental consent, and do not replace any parental authorization required by applicable law.

4. Google Play purchases and Unity IAP

The App uses Unity In-App Purchasing (IAP) 5.5.1 with Google Play for coin packages and Remove Ads. Google Play handles checkout and your payment method. eejsoftflux does not collect or store your payment-card number, bank-account number, or Google account password in the App.

The App receives purchase status, product identifiers, receipts, and transaction tokens to validate purchases, deliver content, avoid duplicate grants, and restore eligible purchases. Receipt-signature checks and the App's fulfillment record are local; they do not eliminate Google Play or Unity SDK communications.

Unity IAP 5.4 and later can process purchase and transaction details, device/application and session identifiers, technical information such as device model, operating system and locale, and country inferred from requests' IP addresses. These support the IAP service, transaction deduplication, and revenue reporting. Unity's SDK can transmit this information to Unity even without a separately integrated Unity Analytics SDK. See Unity IAP privacy information.

Product prices and currency are supplied by Google Play, not inferred from the selected age band. Remove Ads can be restored from eligible Google Play purchases using the same Play account. Already consumed coin purchases and local coin balances are not guaranteed to be recoverable after data deletion or reinstallation.

5. On-device storage

The age band, progress, item counts, preferences, and related game flags are stored using Unity PlayerPrefs. The coin balance, ad-removal status, and fulfilled purchase-token hashes are also stored in an app-local purchase record; PlayerPrefs keeps a compatibility copy of the coin balance.

This data is used to resume play, remember settings, manage inventory, and prevent repeated purchase fulfillment. It is not a developer-managed online account. Local storage is not a guarantee against device access, modification, loss, or backup restoration.

6. Third-party services and information sharing

The current integration includes Google Mobile Ads/AdMob, Google UMP, Google Play billing, Unity IAP, and Google Play In-App Review. No separate Firebase, Crashlytics, or custom gameplay-analytics integration is present in the reviewed game code. Unity engine diagnostics, cloud crash reporting, and automatic Unity Analytics initialization are disabled in the project settings. Before scene loading, the App sets Unity AdsIntent and AnalyticsIntent to Denied for all ages. These settings do not disable essential Unity IAP transaction processing or the independent data handling of Google SDKs. Advertising and purchasing services can still perform their own measurement and diagnostics.

If you choose to submit a Google Play rating or review, Google handles your submission and its publication. The App records that it requested the review flow; the review API does not tell the game which rating or text you submitted.

These providers handle information under their applicable policies and may process it outside your country. For details and provider-specific privacy choices, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy — Google advertising, consent, and Play services.
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice — payment-related information.
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy — Unity service processing.
  • Google Play In-App Review information — optional review submissions.

7. Retention, deletion, and resets

Local game data remains in the App's storage until it is overwritten or removed. To remove it from the current installation, use Android's app settings for Word Match: Tile Puzzle and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Uninstalling ordinarily removes app-local data, but Android/device backup and restore settings can affect whether earlier data returns.

The in-game progress-reset function resets level progress only. It is not a complete deletion of coins, items, purchase records, settings, or information held by third-party providers.

Clearing local data does not delete Google Play transactions or records held by Google or Unity. Their retention and deletion rules apply independently. Unity retains transaction information as needed for service operation, deduplication, and reporting. Use the providers' privacy contacts and account controls for their records.

We cannot remotely erase game data that exists only on your device. If you contact us about privacy, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond and handle the request, and retain it as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Do not send payment-card details or account passwords.

8. Children and age-related privacy

The App is intended for ages 6 and older, including children, teens, and adults; it is not intended for children under 6. On first use, a neutral screen asks you to choose 6–12 years, 13–17 years, or 18 years or older, without a preselected answer or rewards for choosing an older group. It does not ask for a name or date of birth. The age band is saved in local PlayerPrefs and reused on later launches. The corresponding age-treatment signals are provided to advertising and consent SDKs; local storage does not mean those signals remain only on the device.

Settings → Age group allows changes only toward a younger, more protective band: adults may select either younger band, teens may select ages 6–12, and children cannot select an older band. After a change, advertising is stopped for the session and the App must restart to apply the new setting. The App does not automatically advance the saved age band as a user gets older. Contact us if you need help with an incorrect selection; the App does not offer an immediate upgrade to a less protective band.

Parents or guardians may contact eejsoftflux@gmail.com to ask about a child's information, request access or deletion, or report a privacy concern. Please identify the App and describe the request without sending a child's full birth date, identity documents, payment-card details, or passwords. We may ask for limited information needed to verify authority and locate relevant records.

For local data, a parent can use Android's Clear storage/Clear data controls as described above; this also removes the saved age choice and can erase progress and consumables. For provider-held data, we will help identify the appropriate process. Where Unity acts as our processor, requests must be handled through us; where a provider acts independently, its own privacy-request process applies. We cannot promise to erase records that a provider must retain for transactions or legal obligations. The age screen is not a parental-consent system.

9. Your choices

  • Choose whether to watch rewarded ads or make an in-app purchase.
  • Open Settings → Privacy Policy to read this page in your browser. The external website and browser may process connection information under their own policies.
  • Use Settings → Age group to select a more protective age band.
  • Use Settings → Privacy options when available to review Google's consent choices.
  • Use Android's advertising privacy controls to reset or delete the advertising ID where supported. This does not necessarily stop all advertising or other SDK processing.
  • Clear local app storage to remove game data from the current installation, understanding that progress and consumable balances may be lost.
  • Use Google's and Unity's privacy resources for information they hold, or contact us with questions about access, correction, or deletion. Available rights depend on applicable law and the information concerned.
